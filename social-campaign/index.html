The brief

Most people should do nothing. A few can't afford to.

TRICARE Open Season runs Nov. 9 through Dec. 8, 2026, and any change takes effect Jan. 1, 2027. Beneficiaries who are enrolled and happy with their plan keep it automatically. Those who want to switch plans, or move between individual and family enrollment, have 30 days.

The people the campaign most needs to reach are the ones who are eligible but not enrolled. If they do nothing, they can get care only at a military hospital or clinic when space is available. Outside open season, changes require a qualifying life event.

So the campaign has two jobs: get the right people to act, and tell everyone else they can relax.

Who needs to act? Pick a situation.

The campaign

Four channels, one set of facts.

A plan a department-level social team could run: the strategy, a day-by-day calendar, the posts themselves, how success gets measured, which new formats to try and how it all gets coordinated and approved.

Campaign plan, TRICARE Open Season 2026

Get eligible, unenrolled beneficiaries enrolled by Dec. 8, without alarming everyone else

Objective

By Dec. 8, every eligible beneficiary who needs to act knows how, and everyone who doesn't knows they can keep their coverage as is.

Beyond social

The same three messages and dates run in the department's broader program: radio and TV spots for overseas audiences, Service news stories and installation newspapers, and a toolkit for public affairs offices so local posts match the department's.

Calls to action

Every post points to one of two places: the TRICARE Plan Finder to compare plans, or milConnect to enroll or make a change. Individual questions go to the regional contractor, never to a comment thread.

Subject
New formats for the open season campaign: adopt, pilot or skip

I looked at four formats against three tests: does it serve the campaign's goal, will it still matter next year, and can we run it within our approval, accessibility and records rules?

FormatRecommendationWhy
Vertical short-form explainersAdoptOne 60-second edit runs as YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook video. A durable format, not a trend, and it reuses work we're already doing.
Live Q&A with TRICARE expertsPilotRun once in the final week. Answers the questions people actually have, but needs expert time, live moderation and a plan for questions that require personal details.
"Which one are you?" web sorterPilotTurns the carousel into a tool hosted on an official site. Needs an accessibility review and an owner to keep it accurate next year.
AI chatbot for benefits questionsSkip for nowBenefits answers depend on each person's situation, and a wrong answer has real consequences. Privacy and records rules also apply to health questions. Revisit with the health agency once accuracy can be guaranteed.

Barriers and how to clear them

Live events need expert time booked two weeks out. The web sorter needs a content owner at the health agency. Both need comment moderation coverage on evenings and weekends.

Sharing what we learn

After the season, a short lessons-learned brief goes to Service and command social media managers: which formats moved people to act, the reply bank and the decision rules that worked.

Separating tools from trends

Tests
Goal, durability, compliance
  • "Skip" is a real answer. Not every new tool fits a benefits campaign.
  • Pilots have limits: one live event, one sorter, measured before scaling.
  • Lessons go out to the Services so the next campaign starts ahead.

Approval flow

  1. By Oct. 30DraftSocial team drafts the plan, posts, graphics and reply bank.
  2. By Nov. 3Fact checkTRICARE communications confirms every date, term and link.
  3. By Nov. 4ApproveDepartment public affairs approves messages and the reply bank.
  4. Nov. 4Share the toolkitServices and commands get posts, graphics, alt text and the reply bank, five days before launch.
  5. Nov. 9Launch and monitorPosts go live; comments are monitored daily, with same-day escalation for anything off-script.

Rules that keep messaging consistent

Same dates and wording everywhere. Link to TRICARE.mil or milConnect, never to third-party summaries. Never ask for personal information in comments. Anything not in the reply bank goes to TRICARE communications before we answer.
